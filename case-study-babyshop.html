Where it started

Babyshop is one of Landmark Group’s retail brands in the Gulf region, and its shoppers are parents, buying on the app and the website. The brand was getting a revamp, with a new brand UI across the whole experience. Two screens had to catch up: the product page and the basket.

Add one or two sentences on why the business wanted the revamp.

The problem

The product page and the basket had become cluttered. Too much was competing for attention, and the promotions and offers were hard to see, never sitting in line with the rest of the page. For a parent trying to check out quickly, it was not clear what they were choosing or what they were getting.

Add what this cost shoppers or the business, if you know. For example: offers missed, a slower checkout.

Two screens, one story

The same two problems showed up on both screens: too much on the page, and offers that were easy to miss. So we worked through each screen in turn. Pick one to see how it went.

What we wanted

  • Show promotions and offers clearly, in line with the product
  • Clear the clutter, so the things that matter stand out
  • Help parents make conscious choices on the app and the site
