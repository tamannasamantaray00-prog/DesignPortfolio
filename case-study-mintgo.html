The problem

Most money apps ask people to do the work: sort expenses, build spreadsheets, remember due dates. MintGo aims to be a simple, AI-powered personal finance app that does the thinking, across devices, for real people.

What it does

Automatic expense categories

Card swipes and UPI payments are tagged into categories like groceries, travel or impulse buys, so nobody has to sort through statements.

An AI-powered financial plan

A personalised plan built from income, goals and habits. It updates itself and nudges you when you drift off track.

Flexible forecasting

Adaptive forecasts that follow your lifestyle, whether you are planning a trip or covering monthly EMIs.

Money on the go

Spending alerts, goals, budgets and net worth, all from your phone.
